## Prompt comum — facial e bem-estar

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: illustrative photograph for a boutique Brazilian aesthetics clinic landing page service card. Generate ONE standalone horizontal 3:2 high-quality editorial photograph, no collage or split panels. Subject: [SUBJECT]. Shared art direction across all service images: refined, natural, photorealistic, warm cream #f8f6f0, sage and olive accents #414e32, clean bright soft daylight, matte textures, restrained depth of field, professional photographic lighting, realistic anatomy and skin textures, composed elegantly without stock-photo cliches. Framing suitable for a responsive card cropped to about 1.45:1; keep key subject inside central 70% with generous margins. No text, captions, letters, numbers, logo, watermark, labels, photo borders, UI or graphics. Do not portray or identify any specific real person or real clinic. This is conceptual illustrative imagery, not documentation of services actually provided.

## Prompt — dermatofuncional

A clean unbranded white handheld non-invasive ultrasound therapy applicator with its tidy cable resting on neatly folded ivory cotton towels on a pale stone counter; muted sage background, delicate olive branch far in background, soft natural window light. No people or bodies present. Refined clinical still life, realistic professional product photograph, matte textures, cream #f8f6f0 and olive #414e32 palette with subtle warm highlights, restrained depth of field. Keep key objects in central 70% for responsive card cropping. No writing, numbers, labels, logos, text, watermarks, borders, needles, pills, before-after comparisons or UI.
